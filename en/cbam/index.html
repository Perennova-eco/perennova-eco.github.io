      What is checked

      The 16 data points come from point 1.2 of Annex IV to Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547, as listed by cbamguide.com (read on 22 October 2026). Check the Official Journal text before relying on a single point.

      Data pointWhat the tool does
      1–2 · Operator and installation: name, address, CBAM Registry identifier, UN/LOCODE, coordinates of the main emission sourceFlags a missing field, a UN/LOCODE that is not 2 letters + 3 characters, coordinates out of range or with fewer than 6 decimals
      3 · Production process and route of each goodFlags a missing route; flags a CN code that is not 8 digits or not found in our list of CBAM headings (cement, electricity, fertilisers, iron and steel, aluminium, hydrogen)
      4 · Specific direct (and, where required, indirect) embedded emissions per tonne, share on default values, free allocation, benchmarkFlags a missing or non-numeric value, a share outside 0–100 %, a value above 50 tCO2e per tonne (often kilograms entered as tonnes), and missing indirect emissions for cement and fertilisers (goods outside Annex II)
      5–11 · Installation emissions, heat, zero-rated fuels, waste gases, CO2 capture, on-site electricityFlags a point left blank: the supplier should state “none” rather than leave it empty
      12–16 · Precursors: CN code, country, default or actual value, origin installation, reporting periodFlags a precursor without country or basis, and an actual value without its origin installation
      Method and evidenceFlags actual values without a verification report reference, a footprint calculated under the GHG Protocol or ISO 14067 (not accepted as actual values), and a carbon price paid without supporting evidence

      What the tool does not do: it does not verify the emissions themselves, replace an accredited verifier, compute your certificate cost, or submit anything to the CBAM Registry. Tool built with an AI agent; found an error? contact@perennova.eu.
