      How to file an EUDR due diligence statement, in 6 steps
      

      From your supplier's plot file to the reference number on your customs declaration. Each step says who does it: you, your supplier, or this page. Sources linked, read on 27 October 2026.
      

        

      1. 1 · Check that you are the one who files
        You file a due diligence statement if you are the operator who first places the product on the EU market (import or EU production) or exports it: cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya, wood and their listed products. Since the December 2025 amendment, downstream operators and traders no longer file: the first one only collects the reference number from its supplier and keeps it for at least 5 years. Micro and small primary operators in low-risk countries can submit a one-off simplified declaration instead. Dates: 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators, 30 June 2027 for micro and small ones (European Commission).
        You
        2. 

      2. 2 · Get the plot file from each supplier
        The statement carries the geolocation of every plot where the commodity was produced. Ask each supplier now, not in December: create a personal link per supplier; they check and fix their own file here and send it back with a check certificate.
        Your supplier · this page, free
        3. 

      3. 3 · Check and fix every file before upload
        The EU Information System rejects open or self-crossing boundaries, holes, lines, swapped coordinates, invalid country codes, numbers written as text and more than 25 MB of files per statement (GeoJSON File Description). Drop the file above: you get the blocking errors, a fixed GeoJSON (WGS 84, 6 decimals) and a TRACES filing sheet for that file.
        This page · free for one file, Pro or Importer for batches
        4. 

      4. 4 · Assess and reduce the risk
        The Commission classifies each country as low, standard or high risk (Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1093 of May 2025; high: Belarus, Myanmar, North Korea, Russia). For products from a low-risk country, simplified due diligence applies: no risk assessment unless you hold credible information of non-compliance. Otherwise, assess the risk that the product is not deforestation-free since 31 December 2020 or not legally produced, and reduce it before filing. This page does not check deforestation or legality.
        You, or your compliance provider
        5. 

      5. 5 · Register and file in the EU Information System (TRACES)
        Create a personal EU Login account (with an authenticator app, not SMS), then register your company as operator, with its EORI number if you import or export. Practise in the ACCEPTANCE environment, which has no legal value, then file in PRODUCTION. In the statement: activity (import, export, domestic production), HS code, description, net mass in kg, scientific name for wood, the geolocation (import your fixed GeoJSON), and the reference and verification numbers of any upstream statement you rely on. Then submit and sign. The Commission runs free online trainings for operators on 3, 10, 17 and 24 November 2026 at 14:00 CET (Information System page).
        You · the filing sheet lists what your file already answers
        6. 

      6. 6 · Use and keep the reference number
        Once the statement is available, you receive a reference number and a verification number. The reference number goes on the customs declaration and to your buyer; share the verification number only with a company that builds its own statement on yours. Keep the due diligence information for at least 5 years.

      The rules applied
      

      Rejected by TRACES? The fix for each error, one page per error (also in Spanish, Portuguese and French).
      

      They come from the EUDR Information System documentation, “GeoJSON File Description”, read on 14 October 2026.
      

      RuleWhat the tool does
      

      WGS84 coordinates, longitude first, then latitudeFlags out-of-range coordinates; flags a point that falls outside the declared country, and says whether it falls inside once longitude and latitude are swapped
      

      The system truncates to 6 decimals; two points that become identical are an errorTruncates to 6 decimals and removes duplicates
      

      Closed ring: last point = first pointCloses the ring
      

      Polygons with holes and self-intersecting boundaries are rejectedRemoves holes (to be checked); flags self-intersections, to be redrawn
      

      Point, Polygon, MultiPoint, MultiPolygon onlyRejects lines; splits MultiPoints
      

      Property names are case-sensitive (ProducerCountry, ProductionPlace, ProducerName, Area)Renames productionplace, PRODUCERCOUNTRY, etc. to the right case
      

      ProducerCountry as a 2-letter ISO code; Area as a number, in hectaresUpper-cases the code, adds the default country, converts Area to a number
      

      Above 4 hectares a polygon is required; 25 MB per statementFlags points declared above 4 ha, counts polygons under 4 ha and the file size
      

      

      What the tool does not do: it does not check deforestation-free status after 31 December 2020, nor legality of production, nor the upload itself. Area is a spherical estimate, within about 1%. Tool built with an AI agent; found an error? perennova@proton.me.
