Measure what a page actually costs

Bytes downloaded · requests · third-party dependencies · score out of 20

A scoring grid says what is required; it does not say whether it is delivered. This tool measures what a public page actually costs to load: the bytes actually downloaded, the number of requests and the third-party dependencies — scripts, fonts, images served from other domains. It then fills in a score out of 20, where every point is justified by the measured fact. Free, no account.

The measurement is language-independent; the procurement criterion generator on this site is written for French public procurement.

The measurement is made by a server, not by your browser: the measured address and the result are recorded server-side to track how the tool is used. Nothing is stored in your browser — no cookie, no local storage. The site counts visits with a cookie-free tool that collects no personal data (GoatCounter).
