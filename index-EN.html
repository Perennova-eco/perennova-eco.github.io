Where this comes from

It is the free extract of a paid kit that goes further: a 20-criteria scoring grid, a three-line “Impact” block to paste into tickets, a five-slide template for the steering committee, four metrics and four traps. That kit currently exists in French only, so there is no buy link on this page — it would be selling you a document you can't read. If an English edition would be useful to you, say so: perennova@proton.me. That is the whole ask.

This is neither an audit nor a compliance check. It is derived from the French public reference framework for sustainable digital services, the RGESN — 78 criteria, published by Arcep and Arcom with ADEME, free and public: ecoresponsable.numerique.gouv.fr. Start there if your goal is an audit.

The weighting behind the questions leans on the ADEME–Arcep study finding that user devices account for 65–92% of the footprint of digital services — which is why “don't push people to replace their hardware” outranks “optimise the server”.
